NEWS PHOTOS

One folder per post, named after the post's markdown file:

  news/revolute-hackathon/   ->  src/content/blog/revolute-hackathon.md
  news/brh-fk-ik-talk/       ->  src/content/blog/brh-fk-ik-talk.md

Paste photos into the matching folder. They appear under the post, full
width and uncropped, with the filename as the caption. The first photo also
becomes the card image on the News section of the home page.

Adding a new post? Make a folder here with the same name as the .md file.

  01-teams-at-work.jpg   -> first, caption "teams at work", card cover
  02 Judging round.jpg   -> second, caption "Judging round"
  IMG_4821.jpg           -> no caption
